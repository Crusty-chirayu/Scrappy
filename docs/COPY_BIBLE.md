# SCRAPPY Copy Bible

> Voice: plain, specific, calm, slightly dry. Short sentences. No hype. Always explain what happened, why, and what to do next.

---

## 1. Forbidden Words & Phrases (Kill List)

Never use these marketing buzzwords anywhere in code or copy:
- *Seamless*
- *Revolutionize* / *Revolutionary*
- *Unlock the power of*
- *Supercharge*
- *Game-changing*
- *Leverage* (as a verb)
- *Empower*
- *All-in-one platform*
- *Next-generation*
- *Magic* / *Magical*

---

## 2. Source Honesty Phrases

Every price or observation must have clear, honest provenance:
- `"Live, verified 4 min ago"`
- `"Sample data"` (Used strictly for local demos or unverified offline mocks)
- `"Fixture data"`
- `"Source not verified yet"`
- `"Source unavailable"` (When adapter is offline or blocked)

---

## 3. Formatting Standards

- **Prices**: Currency `INR` (`₹`) with Indian numbering system (Lakh/Crore grouping: `₹1,23,456.00`).
- **Paise**: De-emphasized smaller font size.
- **Deltas**: Always paired with sign and direction arrow (`-12% ↓` or `+5% ↑`).
- **Dates & Times**: Relative display (`"verified 8m ago"`) with precise timestamp in tooltip and `aria-label`.
- **Buttons**: Action-oriented verb-first phrases (`"Watch this product"`, `"Compare prices"`, `"Extract dataset"`).
